Child and Women Safety System Using GPS

Automatic Smart Street Lighting System

A smart lighting prototype using ESP32 and IR sensors to adjust lighting based on nearby movement. With suitable light sensing and control, it can reduce unnecessary lighting during daylight or when a road is unoccupied.

ESP32 IR Sensors Automation Energy Efficiency

CARDIO-SHIELD

An embedded health-monitoring prototype using a pulse sensor, Arduino Uno, GPS, and GSM. It can send an alert with location information when the measured pulse crosses configured thresholds. It is not a medically validated diagnostic device.

Arduino Uno Pulse Sensor GPS GSM

Electronic Nose for Food Monitoring

A sensor-based monitoring prototype using MQ-135 and DHT11 to observe selected gas-related and environmental conditions around stored food. The readings can help indicate changes that need further inspection.

MQ-135 DHT11 Sensors Food Monitoring

Electricity Generation Using Footsteps

A prototype that uses piezoelectric sensors to convert pressure from footsteps into electrical energy. Suitable rectification and energy conditioning can help demonstrate the generated output through LEDs or an appropriate storage circuit.
